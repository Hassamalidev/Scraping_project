How collection works

  • Only business listings and business phone numbers. Never private homeowners.
  • Each website is asked politely: its robots.txt rules are followed and requests go at most once a second. If a site refuses, collection stops for that site and says so here.
  • Every worker keeps a link to the record it came from (open a worker → “Where this came from”).
